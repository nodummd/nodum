# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues privately through GitHub:
**[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/nodummd/nodum/security/advisories/new)**.

Do not open a public issue, discussion or forum topic for a vulnerability.

Include what you can of:

- the affected component (API, web app, MCP server, publishing, deploy stack),
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept,
- the impact as you understand it.

We aim to acknowledge a report within three working days and to keep you
updated until it is resolved. Credit is given in the advisory unless you ask
otherwise.

## Supported versions

Security fixes land on `main`, which is what https://nodum.md runs. Self-hosted
deployments should track the latest release.

## Scope

In scope: this repository and the hosted service at nodum.md and its
subdomains. Out of scope: denial of service through volume, social
engineering, and findings in third-party services we do not operate.

The machine-readable contact is at
https://nodum.md/.well-known/security.txt (RFC 9116).
